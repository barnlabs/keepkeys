# KeepKeys brand

KeepKeys is calm, local, and precise. Its identity should feel like a trustworthy
workbench tool, not a security alarm or a cloud-vault product.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep pine | `#1F2D27` | Primary type, shield, dark surfaces |
| Night pine | `#14211D` | Hero background |
| Barn ember | `#D96C4D` | Active state, keyhole, restrained accents |
| Warm paper | `#FFF8EC` | Light surfaces and reverse type |
| Field sage | `#41544C` | Supporting copy |

Use ember as an accent, not a full-page background. Warm paper replaces clinical
white. The hero may use a subtle paper gradient; product icons and wordmarks stay
flat.

## Mark and language

Preserve clear space around the shield equal to one third of its width. Do not
stretch, rotate, recolor, add shadows, or put another lock inside the shield.
Prefer “local secrets for agents” and “use, never retrieve.” Never claim that
KeepKeys is unbreakable, guarantees secrecy, or securely erases Keychain storage
at the forensic layer.
