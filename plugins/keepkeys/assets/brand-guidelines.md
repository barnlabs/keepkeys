# KeepKeys brand

KeepKeys is calm, local, and precise. Its identity should feel like a trustworthy
workbench tool, not a security alarm or a cloud-vault product.

## The Keykeeper

The primary character is the **Keykeeper**: a dignified older man with a silver
beard, deep-pine work coat, barn-ember scarf, and a large ring of aged-brass
keys. He is capable, warm, quietly vigilant, and accountable to the user. He is
not a wizard, pirate, janitor caricature, Santa figure, or faceless corporate
mascot.

The ring is the focal point. It represents named, separate credentials under
one user's control; it does not imply that KeepKeys owns, creates, or can reveal
those credentials.

Primary assets:

- `keykeeper.png`: full-resolution character artwork;
- `social-preview.png`: GitHub/README hero lockup;
- `icon.png`, `logo.png`, and `logo-dark.png`: plugin-store crops.

The character artwork is an original OpenAI-generated illustration produced for
BarnLabs on 2026-07-24. Do not substitute stock art, another product's mascot,
or a recognizable real person.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep pine | `#1F2D27` | Primary type, shield, dark surfaces |
| Night pine | `#14211D` | Hero background |
| Barn ember | `#D96C4D` | Active state, keyhole, restrained accents |
| Aged brass | `#C79A45` | Key ring, metadata labels, restrained highlights |
| Warm paper | `#FFF8EC` | Light surfaces and reverse type |
| Field sage | `#41544C` | Supporting copy |

Use ember as an accent, not a full-page background. Warm paper replaces clinical
white. The hero may use a subtle paper gradient; product icons and wordmarks stay
flat.

## Mark and language

Use the complete Keykeeper illustration for hero, store, social, and
announcement surfaces. At very small sizes, the brass key-ring utility mark may
replace the character, but a shield or padlock must not become the primary
identity.

Preserve the original crop, expression, coat, scarf, and visible ring. Do not
stretch, mirror, recolor, add dramatic shadows, place text over the face or keys,
or make the character ominous or comic.

Prefer “local secrets for agents” and “use, never retrieve.” Never claim that
KeepKeys is unbreakable, guarantees secrecy, or securely erases Keychain storage
at the forensic layer.
